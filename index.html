About Venegaon Cha Raja

Established in 1957, Shri Jay Bhavani Ganesh Mandal, Venegaon, has been a symbol of devotion, unity, and cultural heritage for generations. What began as a humble initiative to unite the village through Lord Ganesha's blessings has grown into one of the region's most respected Ganesh Mandals. Every year, the 11-day Ganeshotsav is celebrated with devotion while preserving traditions and serving society.

For more than seven decades, Shri Jay Bhavani Ganesh Mandal has remained a symbol of faith, unity, and tradition in Venegaon. Every Ganeshotsav brings together thousands of devotees who gather to seek the blessings of Venegaon Cha Raja and celebrate the spirit of togetherness with great devotion.

Beyond the festival, the mandal actively serves society through meaningful initiatives such as tree plantation drives, free health check-up camps, blood donation camps, educational assistance for students, construction of a public bus shelter, and various drawing and cultural competitions for children. These activities reflect our commitment to serving society while keeping our cultural heritage alive.

Our Beginning

Established in 1957, Shri Jay Bhavani Ganesh Mandal was founded with the vision of uniting the people of Venegaon through devotion, preserving our rich cultural traditions, and celebrating Ganeshotsav with faith, harmony, and community spirit.

Our Vision

Our mission is to inspire devotion, preserve cultural heritage, encourage youth participation, and serve society through meaningful initiatives while keeping the values of Lord Ganesha alive for future generations.
